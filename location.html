Hotel:

The primary venue for Middleware 2007 will be the Newport Beach Marriot Hotel & Spa. This hotel is located about 10 minutes from the University of California at Irvine (UCI) campus.

There is a special rate for Middleware 2007 conference which is US$169 per night. You may use     "mdlmdla" group code and click here for reservation with the conference special rate. Parking is not included in this rate. Parking is $16.00-$22.00 a night.

Hotel shuttle is complimentary to & from John Wayne airport. It leaves
the hotel at the top of the hour, every hour. So 6am, 7am and so on.
The shuttle picks up the guests at the airport once an hour, at the
bottom of the hour; at 6:30am, 7:30am and so on. It is on a 1st come,
1st serve basis and also stop at the Marriott Bayview Suites since it is on the way to the airport. It seats about
8-10ppl and stops at the ground transportation area at the airport. The shuttle is white & red with the hotel logo printed on the side.

20 more rooms have been added at the special conference rate for the previous Sunday and following Saturday for those wishing to stay longer.  We may be
able to request more if there is sufficient demand, but that they shouldn't expect slots to be available as we get closer to the conference dates.

Wireless High Speed Internet is available inside the meeting rooms, foyer area & registration desk. Please stop by the Middleware registration table located at the Newport Coast Registration Desk to receive the access code.  If you need Internet access in your room the following option is available:

Wired for Business is available in the guest rooms. For a set daily rate of $12.95 you get 1) wireless high speed internet in the guest room, hotel lobby and Starbucks, 2) unlimited local phone calls, 3) unlimited long distance calls (within the US).
